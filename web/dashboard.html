Output Layer Weights — W3 (1 × 16)
These 16 weights connect the second hidden layer directly to the final sigmoid. They are the most interpretable layer: a positive (red) weight means that input feature increases the predicted engagement score, a negative (blue) weight suppresses it. Bars are grouped by feature category.
Note: These are the output-layer weights only. The actual influence of each input on the prediction also passes through W1 and W2 (the hidden layers), so direct weight magnitude is an approximation of importance — not a perfect measure.
